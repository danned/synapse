# Conductive Terrain

**Focus:** Board identity

## Opportunity

The five levels have different paths and blocked cells, but most buildable cells behave alike. A level could offer a signature routing opportunity.

## Idea

Place one or two visible conductive sockets on each level. A tower built on a socket gains a local effect, such as a faster first outgoing pulse, a wider incoming link, or a brief pulse echo after every fourth arrival. Give each socket one fixed rule and show its effect in the placement preview. Scar tissue and path cells remain blocked as usual.

## Why it helps

Sockets create desirable positions that compete with range and lane coverage. They can make each level memorable without adding another currency or menu.

## Design check

Hand-check that no socket creates an obvious best opening and that ordinary cells remain viable. Use shape and text as well as color to identify sockets.
