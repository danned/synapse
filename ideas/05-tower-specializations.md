# Tower Specializations

**Focus:** Strategic depth and replayability

## Current friction

Every Arc, Cryo, and Lance of a given type behaves alike. Mutation cards change the whole network, but individual tower decisions end at type and placement.

## Idea

Offer each non-Relay tower one of two mutually exclusive specializations after it has participated in several waves. For example, an Arc could favor stronger link shocks or stronger arrival chains. Apply a modest charge cost and show the choice on the node so players can read their network at a glance.

## Why it helps

The same layout can support different builds, and a player can respond to the enemies their network actually struggles with.
