# Relay Routing Controls

**Focus:** Strategic depth

## Current friction

Relays currently send pulses to child branches in round-robin order. Branching offers coverage, but players cannot express which branch matters most when lane pressure changes.

## Idea

Let a selected relay change its child order or set one child as a priority route during build windows. A priority route receives more pulses, but the other children receive fewer; total pulse output stays the same. Show the selected route and its estimated cadence on the network overlay.

## Why it helps

Routing becomes an active network-design choice. Players can favor a dangerous lane while accepting a visible weakness elsewhere.
