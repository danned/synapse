# Network Coverage Preview

**Focus:** Quality of life and strategy

## Current friction

The board previews a prospective parent link, but players must infer which lane it covers and how branching changes pulse frequency. A valid link can still be strategically poor.

## Idea

While previewing a placement or selecting a node, highlight the affected link, its intersection with enemy lanes, and the downstream pulse route. Display a simple cadence estimate for each child branch, with a clear warning when adding a child will split a relay’s pulses. Keep the overlay optional so the board remains readable on mobile.

## Why it helps

Players can make informed placement decisions and learn the game’s central network tradeoff through play rather than trial and error.
