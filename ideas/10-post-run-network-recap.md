# Post-Run Network Recap

**Focus:** Learning and replayability

## Current friction

The results screen reports the level, wave reached, mode, seed, active mutators, and Gene Shards. It does not explain which parts of the network worked or where leaks happened.

## Idea

Add a compact recap showing the final network, damage by tower and link, and leaks by lane. Highlight the most productive branch and the weakest defended section. Keep the recap available after both victory and defeat, alongside the existing replay-this-seed action.

## Why it helps

Players can connect outcomes to their design choices, then use the same seed to test a better layout.
