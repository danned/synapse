# Telegraphed Boss Phases

**Focus:** Climactic waves

## Opportunity

The Severer is dangerous because it repeatedly disables crossed links, but its fight does not yet ask the player to read and answer changing phases.

## Idea

Give major bosses a short cycle of readable intentions: charge a disruption, sever a highlighted link region, then expose a vulnerable window. Display the targeted region and countdown on the board. During exposure, timely pulse arrivals can interrupt the next disruption. Add one distinct phase pattern to later boss encounters instead of merely raising health.

## Why it helps

Bosses become tense routing puzzles with moments of anticipation and payoff. The player can attribute a win or loss to a visible decision.

## Design check

Keep the fight beatable without an active ability, and avoid targeting a link the player cannot reasonably protect or recover.
