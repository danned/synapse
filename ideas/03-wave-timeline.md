# Wave Timeline

**Focus:** Quality of life

## Current friction

The existing Next Signal panel lists enemy types and lane counts, but it does not show whether threats arrive as a rush, a lull, or a late boss. Timing matters because links only act when pulses pass through them.

## Idea

Add a compact left-to-right arrival timeline above the board or within the intel panel. Mark lane, enemy type, and dense spawn clusters. Respect the current difficulty rules: easy shows exact composition and timing, normal shows broad clusters, and hardcore shows only coarse pressure and the existing boss warning.

## Why it helps

The timeline gives players a useful planning tool without erasing the information differences between difficulty modes.
