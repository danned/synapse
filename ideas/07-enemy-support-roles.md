# Enemy Support Roles

**Focus:** Combat depth

## Current friction

Current enemies test speed, health, damage resistance, and link disruption. Most can be handled by improving overall damage along a lane.

## Idea

Introduce a low-health support enemy that briefly shields nearby allies or boosts their speed while it lives. Make its aura obvious on the board and expose its presence through the existing difficulty-specific wave intel. Keep its effect local so positioning and pulse timing determine whether it can be intercepted.

## Why it helps

Players gain a reason to shape links for early interception and to consider which lane needs concentrated damage first.
