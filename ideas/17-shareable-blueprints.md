# Shareable Blueprints

**Focus:** Community and replay

## Opportunity

Replay This Seed repeats a run's setup, but there is no quick way to show another player how a network was built or challenge them to improve it.

## Idea

Export a compact code containing the level, seed, difficulty, mutators, loadout, and final tower placements and links. Importing it starts the same run with the build shown as a translucent ghost, not as free towers. Players can follow, adapt, or ignore the ghost. Mark specializations and draft cards as annotations rather than granting unavailable progression.

## Why it helps

Sharing creates concrete design challenges and makes clever networks teachable. It works through copy and paste, without accounts or a server.

## Design check

Version and validate codes, reject invalid links safely, and keep imported builds playable when the recipient has fewer unlocks.
