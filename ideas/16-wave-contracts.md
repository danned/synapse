# Wave Contracts

**Focus:** Player-chosen risk

## Opportunity

Optional objectives add goals to selected waves, but players cannot choose the kind of pressure they want to face for a better reward.

## Idea

At a few build breaks, offer two visible contracts for the next wave. Each adds a specific enemy twist, such as an early Skitter rush or one armored escort, and promises a fixed bonus if the wave is survived. The player may decline both. Show the chosen change in the wave intel and record the selection for replaying that run.

## Why it helps

The player can steer a run toward the threats their network handles well, or take a risk to fund a stronger next build. The choice makes preparation more personal.

## Design check

Keep contracts separate from optional objective rewards so the two systems remain understandable. Never hide the added threat on Hardcore after the player has selected it.
