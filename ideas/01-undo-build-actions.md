# Undo Build Actions

**Focus:** Quality of life

## Current friction

Placing a tower takes two taps, but recycling a mistaken placement returns only part of its cost. A mistaken rewire must be corrected manually, and recycling a node removes its entire downstream branch at once.

## Idea

Add an **Undo** button during build windows. It reverses the most recent placement, rewire, or branch sale, including the exact charge change and all affected nodes. Clear the undo history when a wave launches so combat never has to rewind. Show the pending action in the button label, such as “Undo sale: 3 nodes.”

## Why it helps

Players can experiment with topology and recover from touch mistakes without weakening a run. The feature preserves the cost of deliberate redesign once combat begins.
