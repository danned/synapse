# Network Puzzle Missions

**Focus:** Handcrafted challenges

## Opportunity

Campaign and Endless test long-run planning. Neither offers a short, focused challenge that teaches one surprising network interaction.

## Idea

Add a set of compact puzzle missions with fixed maps, manifests, charge budgets, and a small allowed tower list. Give each a clear objective, such as stopping a rush with one relay and two towers or defeating a shielded escort with a single lane crossing. Award a completion badge and optional efficiency medal; keep the normal campaign progression independent.

## Why it helps

Players get memorable five-minute challenges that teach routing, pulse cadence, and enemy counters through success. Fixed setups also make scores comparable.

## Design check

Every mission should have multiple valid solutions. Reveal enough information to reason about the puzzle before launching the wave.
