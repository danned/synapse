# Optional Wave Objectives

**Focus:** Variety and player choice

## Current friction

The current wave goal is always survival. Good networks may finish several waves without asking the player to change tactics.

## Idea

Offer one optional objective before selected waves, such as defeating a marked enemy before it reaches the midpoint or preventing any leaks on one lane. Completing it grants a small charge bonus; failing it does not end the run or reduce the normal wave reward. Present the goal before launch and track it in the HUD.

## Why it helps

Objectives create short-term tactical puzzles and give confident players a voluntary reason to adjust their network.
