# Audio sources

All bundled audio is released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0, permitting commercial use and redistribution without required attribution: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- Music and victory cue: SRG774, [Dark Sci-Fi Audio Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/dark-sci-fi-audio-pack), CC0. Selected `airy.ogg`, `pulse.ogg`, `urgent.ogg`, `victory.ogg` from `ogg.zip`. The files have been moved and renamed only as needed for the game.
- Sci-fi effects: Kenney, [Sci-fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds), CC0. Selected `forceField_000.ogg`, `forceField_001.ogg`, `laserSmall_000.ogg`, `laserLarge_000.ogg`, `explosionCrunch_000.ogg`, `lowFrequency_explosion_000.ogg`, and `computerNoise_000.ogg` from `sci-fi_sounds.zip`.
- Interface effects: Kenney, [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), CC0. Selected `click_001.ogg`, `confirmation_001.ogg`, `confirmation_002.ogg`, `confirmation_003.ogg`, `select_001.ogg`, `switch_001.ogg`, `minimize_001.ogg`, and `error_001.ogg` from `kenney_interfaceSounds.zip`.
